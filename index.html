JEE 2027 Admission & Counselling Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough of the seat allocation cycle for IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.

1

Exam & Results Declaration

Candidates appear for the JEE Main (Session 1 & 2) and/or JEE Advanced exams. NTA declares shift-wise normalized percentile scores, and subsequently publishes the Final All India Rank (AIR) list based on the best of two JEE Main session percentiles. The top ~2.5 Lakh candidates qualify for JEE Advanced.

2

JoSAA Portal Registration & Choice Filling

All qualified candidates register on the official JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) portal. Here, candidates must lock their preferences for engineering colleges and branches. Option-filling is a critical step; candidates must enter choices in order of absolute preference, as reallocation only happens upward.

3

Mock Seat Allocation & Rounds

JoSAA releases mock seat allocations to help students gauge their probability. Next, actual seat allocation happens over multiple rounds (typically 5-6 rounds). When a seat is allocated, the candidate must choose one of three options: Freeze (accept and lock), Slide (accept but look for higher branch in the same institute), or Float (accept but look for higher branch/institute).

4

Document Verification & Seat Acceptance

Once a seat is accepted, candidates upload required academic and category documents (like Category Certificate, Class 10/12 marksheets, Medical Certificate) and pay the seat acceptance fee. Verification is conducted online by the virtual reporting center of the allotted institute.

5

CSAB Special Rounds (For NITs, IIITs & GFTIs)

After JoSAA rounds conclude, any vacant seats remaining in NITs, IIITs, and other Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs) are filled via two CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) Special Rounds. CSAB rounds often see cutoffs drop, giving a second chance to students with lower percentiles.

📊 Category Reservation Quotas

Seat allocation in JoSAA and CSAB strictly adheres to Government of India reservation mandates:

General (OPEN) 40.5%
OBC-NCL 27.0%
Scheduled Caste 15.0%
Gen-EWS 10.0%
Scheduled Tribe 7.5%

* Note: A horizontal reservation of 5% is allocated for Persons with Disabilities (PwD) within each respective category. Supernumerary female seats (~20%) are created to improve gender diversity at IITs & NITs.

JoSAA & CSAB Counselling Roadmap 2027

A step-by-step breakdown of how seat allocation works from rank release to final campus reporting.

1

JoSAA Registration & Choice Filling

After JEE Advanced results, candidates register on JoSAA portal and submit their ordered list of preferred institute and branch combinations across 23 IITs, 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and 38 GFTIs.

2

Mock Seat Allotment (Rounds 1 & 2)

JoSAA releases two mock seat allocations to help candidates understand their seat probability based on current choice lists and refine their preferences before locking.

3

6 Official Rounds of Allotment

JoSAA conducts 6 main seat allocation rounds. Upon receiving an offer, candidates must choose between Freeze (accept & lock), Float (accept & upgrade anywhere), or Slide (accept & upgrade within same institute).

4

Online Document Verification & Fee Acceptance

Candidates must upload required certificates (12th Marksheet, Category Certificate, State Domicile proof) and pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) online.

5

CSAB Special Rounds (NITs/IIITs/GFTIs)

Vacant seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs after Round 6 are filled via 2 Special Rounds conducted by CSAB. Ranks up to 1,50,000+ often clear cutoffs in CSAB rounds.

Official Examination Methodology

Understanding NTA Normalization & Percentile Scores

How the National Testing Agency calculates multi-shift equi-percentile rankings to ensure complete fairness across paper difficulties.

The Official Mathematical Formula

The NTA Percentile score indicates the percentage of candidates who have scored EQUAL TO OR BELOW (same or lower raw marks) that particular candidate in that session:

Percentile Score (P) = 100 × (m / N)
  • m: Number of candidates who appeared in the session with raw score ≤ candidate's score.
  • N: Total number of candidates who appeared in that specific session.
  • Percentiles are calculated to 7 decimal places to prevent tie clustering.

Shift Difficulty Balancing Example

Consider two aspirants taking exams of drastically different difficulty levels:

Candidate A (Hard Shift - e.g., April 4 Shift 2):
Scores 175 Marks out of 300. Because the paper was tough, 99.15% of candidates in that shift scored ≤ 175. Percentile = 99.1500000%.
Candidate B (Easy Shift - e.g., April 6 Shift 2):
Scores 208 Marks out of 300. Because the paper was easy, only 99.15% of candidates scored ≤ 208. Percentile = 99.1500000%.

Despite a 33-mark raw difference, both earn an identical percentile because normalization adjusts for shift difficulty.

Official NTA Tie-Breaking Rules

When two or more candidates obtain identical total NTA percentiles, ties are broken in this strict order:

  1. Mathematics Percentile: Higher NTA score in Maths receives higher rank.
  2. Physics Percentile: Higher NTA score in Physics.
  3. Chemistry Percentile: Higher NTA score in Chemistry.
  4. Accuracy Ratio: Higher ratio of correct answers to incorrect attempts across the paper.
  5. Subject-Specific Accuracy: Less incorrect answers in Maths, then Physics, then Chemistry.
  6. Age (Older): Candidate older in age is awarded the higher rank.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about JEE Main 2027 normalizations, calculations, and college selections.

How does JEE Main normalization work? ▼
Since the exam is conducted in multiple shifts with varying difficulty, NTA uses a normalization process based on percentile scores. Your percentile represents the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or less than you in that specific shift. It is calculated as: 100 × (No. of candidates in shift with score ≤ raw score) / (Total candidates in shift).
What is a good percentile in JEE Main 2027? ▼
A percentile above 99 is considered excellent and generally guarantees a Computer Science (CSE) seat in top-tier NITs and IIITs. Percentiles between 95 and 98 qualify you for middle and lower-tier NITs/IIITs or core branches. A percentile above 93.5 typically qualifies general candidates for JEE Advanced.
How are Category Ranks estimated? ▼
Category ranks (OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST) are estimated from your predicted All India Rank (AIR) using historical allocation curves and the statutory reservation distribution ratios defined by the Government of India (OBC-NCL: 27%, SC: 15%, Gen-EWS: 10%, ST: 7.5%). In counselling portals like JoSAA, category cutoffs are displayed using Category Ranks.
What is the difference between JoSAA and CSAB? ▼
JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) conducts the first 6 rounds of unified counselling for IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. After JoSAA concludes, CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) conducts special rounds to fill vacant seats exclusively in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs (IIT seats are not part of CSAB). CSAB cutoffs are often slightly more relaxed.
How does NTA calculate my final rank if I appear in both Session 1 and Session 2? ▼
If you appear in both January (Session 1) and April (Session 2) examinations, NTA compiles your best overall NTA percentile score among the two sessions. Your final All India Rank is determined solely using your highest percentile score across all candidates.
What is the 75% eligibility criteria for NITs, IIITs, and IITs in 2027? ▼
To secure admission to NITs, IIITs, and IITs through JoSAA, candidates must score at least 75% aggregate marks in their Class 12 Board Examination (65% for SC/ST/PwD candidates) or be within the top 20 percentile of their respective qualifying board.
Can I get a top NIT with a 98 percentile? ▼
Yes. A 98 percentile corresponds to an All India Rank around 25,000 to 30,000. While top 3 NITs (Trichy, Surathkal, Warangal) close CSE under AIR 4,000, a 98 percentile comfortably secures electrical, mechanical, and chemical branches at premier NITs, or Computer Science at renowned newer IIITs and prestigious state institutes.
What are the Float, Slide, and Freeze options in JoSAA counselling? ▼
Freeze accepts the allocated seat and withdraws from further rounds. Float accepts the current seat but allows you to be upgraded to any higher preference institute in subsequent rounds. Slide accepts the seat but allows upgrade only to a higher preference branch within the same institute.
