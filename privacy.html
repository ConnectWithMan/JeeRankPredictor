Privacy Policy

At JeeRankPredictor, we are committed to protecting candidate privacy and maintaining full transparency.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We firmly believe that educational calculators should be accessible anonymously. We do not require account registration, mobile phone numbers, roll numbers, or personal identifying credentials to use our JEE Main rank predictor, percentile predictor, or college predictor tools.

2. Information Processed Locally

When you input raw marks, percentile scores, category preferences, or home state selections into our calculators, this data is processed client-side within your browser to compute estimates. We do not store or transmit your test scores to external databases or third-party marketing firms.

3. Browser Local Storage & Cookies

We use standard browser local storage (`localStorage`) solely to store user UI preferences (such as table filters or selected home state) for convenience across page visits. You can clear your browser storage at any time via your browser settings.

4. Web Analytics & Aggregated Logs

To improve performance and site speed, we utilize privacy-preserving web analytics (such as anonymous page view counters) that collect non-identifiable technical data including IP addresses, device browser type, operating system, and referral URLs.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to official educational portals including JoSAA (`josaa.nic.in`), CSAB (`csab.nic.in`), and NTA (`jeemain.nta.ac.in`). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external government portals.
