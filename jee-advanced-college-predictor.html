Branch Specialization & Curriculum Guide

Understand the coursework and career landscape before finalizing your JoSAA choice preference sheet.

💻
Computer Science & Engg (CSE)
The universally contested flagship branch. Covers theoretical computation, OS, distributed cloud architecture, AI/ML, and compilers. Closes within AIR 60 to 600 in the top 7 IITs, commanding the highest starting domestic and international placement compensation packages.
📐
Mathematics & Computing (MnC)
A rigorous blend of abstract mathematics, algorithmic game theory, stochastic calculus, and computer programming. MnC graduates are aggressively recruited by quantitative hedge funds, high-frequency trading (HFT) desks, and cryptography labs at packages matching or exceeding CSE.
⚡
Electrical & Electronics (EE / ECE)
Encompasses microelectronics, VLSI hardware synthesis, semiconductor design, embedded robotics, and signal processing. With India's semiconductor mission and global chip fab investments, EE/ECE is experiencing explosive demand while maintaining eligibility for over 85% of tech software jobs.
⚙️
Core Disciplines (Mech, Chem, Civil)
Foundational engineering covering thermodynamics, aerospace propulsion, energy transition, and infrastructure. Offers deep exposure to industrial automation, electric vehicles (EV), and high-paying quantitative management consulting (BCG, McKinsey, Bain).

⚠️ Crucial JoSAA Advice: Never Gamble on Branch Sliding

Because freshman grading at IITs operates on rigorous relative grading against India's sharpest academic minds, fewer than 2% to 4% of students successfully slide into CSE or ECE. Never enroll in a branch you dislike under the optimistic assumption that you will change it later. Always choose a branch whose curriculum you are eager to study for four full years.

Zero Home State Quota & JoSAA Seat Mechanics

IIT admissions in JoSAA follow distinct national guidelines that differentiate them entirely from NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.

100% All India Quota (AI) — Zero Domicile Reservation

In the NIT system, 50% of total seats are reserved exclusively for candidates who completed Class 12 within the home state of that institute. In the 23 IITs, exactly 0% of seats are reserved for home states. All ~17,500+ IIT undergraduate seats are allocated strictly on an All India Quota (AI) basis. A student from Maharashtra has identical statistical odds at IIT Bombay as a student from Bihar or Tamil Nadu with the same rank.

JoSAA Withdrawal Deadline & The CSAB Exclusion Rule

Candidates who wish to withdraw their allotted IIT seat and receive a refund of their seat acceptance fee must complete the online withdrawal process before the penultimate round of JoSAA counselling (prior to Round 5 / Round 6). Once the final round allocation is generated, IIT seats cannot be surrendered. Vacant IIT seats DO NOT transfer to CSAB Special Rounds—any seat remaining unoccupied after JoSAA simply lapses!

IIT Tuition Fee Remission & Scholarship Rules

Official Ministry of Education (MoE) fee waiver guidelines for undergraduate B.Tech / Dual Degree students.

💎
100% Tuition Fee Remission (Free Tuition)
SC, ST, and PwD students receive a 100% complete waiver of the tuition fee, regardless of their family income. Additionally, General, EWS, and OBC students whose parental annual income is less than ₹1,00,000 (INR 1 Lakh) per annum receive a full 100% tuition fee waiver.
🪙
66.67% (2/3rd) Tuition Fee Remission
General, EWS, and OBC-NCL students whose family gross annual income is between ₹1,00,000 and ₹5,00,000 receive a two-thirds (66.67%) remission of tuition fees. They pay only one-third of the tuition fee (~₹33,333 per semester instead of the standard ₹1,00,000).
🏛️
Full Tuition Fee Tier (> ₹5 Lakhs)
Students from the General, EWS, and OBC categories with family gross income exceeding ₹5,00,000 per annum pay the standard tuition fee of ~₹1,00,000 per semester (~₹2 Lakhs per year). Note that hostel, mess, and gymkhana dues are separate and payable by all students.
🏦
SBI Scholar Collateral-Free Loans
Every IIT student is pre-approved for the SBI Scholar Loan Scheme with zero collateral, zero guarantor requirement, and concessional interest rates. Loans cover up to ₹40 Lakhs including 100% tuition, hostel, mess, and laptop purchase costs with repayment starting 1 year post-graduation.

Female Supernumerary Quota & Seat Allocation Rules

Understanding the 20% gender diversity pool and JoSAA Freeze, Float, and Slide mechanisms.

👩‍🎓
20% Female Supernumerary Pool
To achieve minimum 20% female enrollment, IITs create supernumerary seats without decreasing gender-neutral seats. Female candidates are automatically evaluated first in the Gender-Neutral pool. If not allotted, they compete in the exclusive Female-Only pool, where cutoffs are typically 25% to 40% more relaxed!
🔒
Freeze Option
Selecting Freeze means you accept the currently allocated institute and branch with complete satisfaction. You will not participate in subsequent rounds and your seat is permanently secured.
🚀
Float Option
Selecting Float preserves your current seat as a guaranteed safety backup while keeping you eligible for higher-preference choices across any institute in subsequent JoSAA rounds. If a higher choice is allotted, the previous seat is automatically transferred.
🔀
Slide Option
Selecting Slide holds your allocated seat while allowing upward migration into a higher-preference academic program within the exact same IIT. If no higher branch in that institute opens up, your initial branch is preserved.

IIT Predictor Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers covering female supernumerary seats, fee waivers, branch sliding, and JoSAA IIT counselling.

What are preparatory courses at IITs? ▼
If seats reserved for SC, ST, or PwD candidates remain vacant after all rounds of allocation, IITs fill them with candidates from those categories who cleared relaxed score margins. These candidates undergo a one-year preparatory course at an IIT. Upon passing this program, they are registered directly into the first year of their regular B.Tech course without re-appearing for JEE Advanced.
How does the Female Supernumerary Quota work at IITs? ▼
To improve gender balance to at least 20%, supernumerary seats are created across every branch at each IIT specifically for female candidates. Female applicants are first considered in the gender-neutral pool based on merit. If they do not secure a seat there, they are automatically placed into the female-only merit pool, resulting in significantly relaxed cutoffs (often 1,000 to 4,000 ranks broader).
Are vacant IIT seats filled in CSAB Special Rounds? ▼
No. CSAB special rounds are conducted exclusively for NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. The Indian Institutes of Technology do not participate in CSAB. Any seat in an IIT that remains vacant after the final round of JoSAA counselling cannot be filled and permanently lapses.
Can I change my branch after 1st year at IIT? ▼
It depends on the specific institute. Several premier IITs—including IIT Bombay, IIT Madras, IIT Hyderabad, and IIT Jammu—have formally discontinued branch changes to relieve freshman academic anxiety. Other IITs (like IIT Delhi, IIT Kanpur, IIT Roorkee, and IIT Kharagpur) still allow branch sliding, but require exceptionally high 1st-year CGPA (typically 8.5 to 9.8). Students should choose their initial branch assuming no branch change will occur.
What is the advantage of 5-Year Dual Degree programs at IITs? ▼
Integrated 5-Year B.Tech + M.Tech Dual Degree programs typically close 1,000 to 2,500 ranks lower than equivalent 4-year B.Tech programs in the same IIT. They offer a strategic pathway into Tier-1 campuses like Bombay, Delhi, and Kharagpur, while providing an extra year for high-impact research, internships, and masters-level thesis projects.
Is there any Home State Quota in IITs? ▼
No. All seats in all 23 IITs are allocated solely under the All India (AI) Quota. There is zero Home State, provincial, or domicile reservation in any IIT campus. Every candidate nationwide is evaluated on identical All India merit.
Who is eligible for 100% IIT tuition fee waiver / remission? ▼
Under Ministry of Education rules, 100% tuition fee remission is granted to: (1) All SC, ST, and PwD candidates regardless of parental income, and (2) General, EWS, and OBC candidates whose gross family annual income is below ₹1,00,000 (INR 1 Lakh). Students from families earning between ₹1 Lakh and ₹5 Lakhs receive a two-thirds (66.67%) tuition fee waiver.
What is the JoSAA seat withdrawal deadline for IITs? ▼
Candidates allocated an IIT seat who wish to withdraw and obtain a refund of their seat acceptance fee must do so before the final round of JoSAA counselling (typically before Round 5 or Round 6, depending on the schedule). Once the final round begins, withdrawal from IIT seats is barred by JoSAA rules, and the fee is non-refundable.
Can I accept an IIT seat and still participate in CSAB for NITs? ▼
If you accept and retain an IIT seat through the final JoSAA round, you can still register for CSAB Special Rounds for NITs/IIITs. However, if you are allotted a new seat in CSAB, your previously confirmed IIT seat is permanently forfeited and cannot be reclaimed.
Which is better: Lower branch in Top 7 IITs vs CSE in Tier-2/3 IIT or Top NIT? ▼
If you are committed to software development or AI research, choosing CSE/MnC at a Top NIT (like NIT Trichy or Surathkal) or Tier-2 IIT (like IIT Indore or IIT BHU) offers direct technical curriculum alignment. However, if you are open to quantitative finance, management consulting, product management, or startups, the peer network, alumni influence, and institutional brand of the Top 7 IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Madras, Kanpur) provide unmatched lifetime career leverage.
