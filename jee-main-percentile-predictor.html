Percentile Predictor FAQs

Common questions about NTA normalization, percentiles, and ranks.

How is NTA Percentile calculated? ▼
The NTA percentile indicates the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or below your raw score in your specific shift. It is calculated using: 100 × (Candidates with score ≤ raw score) / (Total candidates in shift).
Why does the same marks give different percentiles in different shifts? ▼
Exam shifts vary in difficulty level. In a tougher shift, candidates average lower raw scores, so a lower score achieves a high percentile. In an easier shift, scores are higher overall, requiring more marks for the same percentile.
What percentile is required to qualify for JEE Advanced 2027? ▼
Typically, the qualifying cutoff percentiles for JEE Advanced are: General (CRL): ~93.2%, General-EWS: ~81.3%, OBC-NCL: ~79.6%, SC: ~60.1%, and ST: ~46.7%.
