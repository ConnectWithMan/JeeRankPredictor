Examination Fundamentals

What is JEE Main?

The premier national-level gateway for undergraduate engineering and technical architecture in India.

The Joint Entrance Examination (Main), conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA), serves as the singular entrance test for admission to undergraduate engineering (B.E./B.Tech), architecture (B.Arch), and planning (B.Planning) programs across 31 National Institutes of Technology (NITs), 26 Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs), and 38 Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs). In addition, JEE Main functions as the exclusive eligibility screening gateway for JEE Advanced, which opens admissions to the prestigious 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

⚡
Dual Session Structure
  • Session 1 (January): First opportunity; allows students to experience CBT exam pressure before Class 12 board examinations.
  • Session 2 (April): Second chance post board exams; lets candidates improve performance without dropping a year.
  • Best-of-Two Scoring: If you take both sessions, NTA automatically selects your highest total percentile score for compiling All India Rank (AIR).
  • Fee Flexibility: Candidates can choose to register for Session 1, Session 2, or both sessions concurrently.
📋
Three Distinct Papers
  • Paper 1 (B.E. / B.Tech): Computer-Based Test (CBT) covering Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (300 Marks).
  • Paper 2A (B.Arch): Mathematics and Aptitude via CBT, with a Pen-and-Paper Drawing Test on drawing sheets (400 Marks).
  • Paper 2B (B.Planning): Mathematics, General Aptitude, and Planning-based objective test (400 Marks).
  • Candidates can opt to write any combination of papers according to their degree goals.

🎯
Marking Rules & Penalties
  • +4 Marks awarded for every correct answer in both Section A and Section B.
  • -1 Mark deducted for every incorrect response in Section A AND Section B (Numerical value).
  • 0 Marks for unattempted or marked-for-review questions.
  • Numerical Answering: Answers must be entered using the on-screen virtual keyboard rounded off to the nearest integer.

Duration & Mediums
  • Time Allowed: 3 Hours (180 minutes) without any sectional time limits. Candidates can navigate freely between PCM subjects.
  • PwD Allocation: 4 Hours (240 minutes) with 20 minutes compensatory time per hour.

  • In case of translation ambiguity, the English version is legally decisive.

Eligibility Criteria & The 75% Rule

Crucial board percentages, subject combinations, and attempt limitations every candidate must know.

📜
Academic Qualifications
  • Qualifying Exam: Must have passed Class 12 or equivalent exam from a recognized state or central board (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, NIOS).
  • Compulsory Subjects: Physics and Mathematics are mandatory, along with any one of Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, or a Technical Vocational subject.
  • Appearing Candidates: Students appearing for their Class 12 board examinations in the exam year are fully eligible.
  • No Age Bar: There is no upper or lower age limit specified by NTA for writing the exam.
⚠️
The 75% JoSAA Admission Criteria
  • General / OBC-NCL / EWS: Must achieve at least 75% aggregate marks in Class 12 OR rank within the Top 20 Percentile of their respective board.
  • SC / ST / PwD Candidates: Minimum required aggregate is relaxed to 65%.
  • Exam vs Admission: You can take JEE Main and get a valid percentile without 75%, but JoSAA will not allot NIT/IIIT/CFTI seats without meeting this threshold.
  • 5 Subjects Counted: Physics, Chemistry, Math, one Language, and fifth highest scoring subject.

NTA Percentile & Normalization Engine

Why raw marks are never comparable across shifts and how equi-percentile scoring ensures fairness.

Because JEE Main is conducted in multiple shifts across multiple days, papers cannot be completely identical in difficulty. To prevent students in difficult shifts from being penalized and students in easier shifts from receiving an unfair advantage, NTA employs the Equi-Percentile Normalization Method.

NTA Percentile = 100 × [ (Candidates in your shift with raw score ≤ your score) ÷ (Total candidates in your shift) ]
Calculated to 7 decimal places (e.g., 99.8451293) to eliminate score bunching and resolve rank ties accurately.
⚖️
Shift Difficulty Offsets
  • Tough Shifts: An aspirant scoring 185 marks on a hard shift can attain a 99.3 percentile.
  • Easy Shifts: In an easy shift, the same 185 marks might yield only a 98.1 percentile.
  • Relative Rank: Percentile measures your relative rank among shift peers, not raw score percentages.
🔀
Official Tie-Breaking Hierarchy
  • 1. Higher NTA Percentile score in Mathematics.
  • 2. Higher NTA Percentile score in Physics.
  • 3. Higher NTA Percentile score in Chemistry.
  • 4. Candidate with lower proportion of attempted incorrect answers.
  • 5. Candidate older in age (or ascending order of application number).

Admissions Architecture

JoSAA & CSAB Counseling Roadmap

The centralized multi-round seat allocation pipeline for securing your engineering seat.

1
Registration & Choice Locking
Enter and reorder unlimited choices of institute and branch combinations on the official JoSAA portal.
2
Mock Seat Allocations
Two simulated mock rounds give insight into probable allotments based on current applicant choices.
3
6 JoSAA Allotment Rounds
Sequential seat allotments using the Gale-Shapley matching algorithm across 118 central institutes.
4
Freeze, Float, or Slide
Choose whether to accept the seat (Freeze), seek a higher branch anywhere (Float), or higher branch in the same college (Slide).
5
CSAB Special Rounds
Two mop-up rounds conducted after JoSAA to fill vacated or leftover seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.
🏛️
Home State vs Other State Quotas
  • In all 31 NITs, exactly 50% of seats are reserved for students who completed their Class 12 in the same state (Home State / HS Quota).
  • The remaining 50% are filled through the Other State (OS) quota.
  • Home State cutoffs are substantially more lenient in states like Goa, Northeast states, and Puducherry.
  • IIITs generally fill 100% seats on an All India Quota (AI) basis without state reservations.
👥
Category Reservations
  • General-EWS: 10% constitutional reservation (Certificate issued on or after April 1 of exam year required).
  • OBC-NCL: 27% reservation for non-creamy layer candidates listed in the Central OBC list.
  • SC / ST: 15% and 7.5% respective reservations across all participating institutions.
  • Female Supernumerary: Minimum 20% female enrollment quota in NITs and IITs.
