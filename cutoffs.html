Counseling Dynamics

JoSAA vs. CSAB Cutoff Expansion Strategy

Understanding Opening vs Closing ranks and how choices shift between counseling phases.

🎯 Opening Rank (OR) vs. Closing Rank (CR)

• Opening Rank (OR): The All India Rank of the very first candidate admitted into that branch in Round 1.

• Closing Rank (CR): The All India Rank of the final candidate to secure the last remaining seat in Round 6.

Rule of Thumb: When assessing admission safety, compare your rank against the Closing Rank. If your rank is within 90% of the CR, your admission chance is high.

⚡ Freeze vs. Float vs. Slide

• Freeze: You accept your allocated college and exit counseling.

• Float (Recommended): You hold your current seat as a guaranteed backup while competing for higher preferences across ALL colleges.

• Slide: You hold your seat and allow upgrades ONLY within the SAME institute for superior branch branches.

🚀 CSAB Special Rounds Opportunity

After JoSAA completes, unoccupied seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs are auctioned in two CSAB Special Rounds.

Why Cutoffs Spike: Many candidates surrender seats to join BITS Pilani or state institutions. Mid-tier NIT branch cutoffs regularly expand by 10,000 to 30,000 ranks.

Got Questions?

Cutoff Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about opening & closing ranks, quotas, and seat allocation rules.

What is the difference between Qualifying Cutoff and Admission Cutoff? ▾

The Qualifying Cutoff is the minimum NTA percentile score required in JEE Main to qualify to appear for JEE Advanced. The Admission Cutoff refers to the actual Opening and Closing Ranks required to gain admission into specific engineering programs at NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and IITs through JoSAA counseling.

How much do cutoffs relax between JoSAA Round 1 and Round 6? ▾

Cutoff ranks expand progressively across rounds as students decline offers, migrate to IITs, or withdraw for state engineering seats. In premier programs like Computer Science at NIT Trichy or Surathkal, cutoffs move by 200–500 ranks. In mid-tier NITs and branches like Civil or Chemical, closing ranks regularly expand by 3,000 to 8,000 ranks between Round 1 and Round 6.

Can an OBC-NCL or Gen-EWS candidate claim an OPEN seat in JoSAA? ▾

Yes, absolutely. All candidates, irrespective of category, are first evaluated against the OPEN (unreserved) merit list. If a reserved category candidate's CRL rank is high enough, they are allocated an OPEN seat, keeping their category quota seat available for other candidates from their community.

How is Home State (HS) quota verified during NIT admissions? ▾

Home State quota eligibility is determined strictly by the location of the school or examination center where you passed your Class 12 board examination, NOT your birthplace or permanent residential domicile.

Do female candidates get separate cutoff ranks in JoSAA? ▾

Yes. Under the Government of India mandate ensuring at least 20% female enrollment in IITs and NITs, JoSAA publishes separate Female-Only Supernumerary Closing Ranks alongside Gender-Neutral ranks. These cutoffs are typically 15% to 40% more relaxed for female applicants.

Is it possible to enter top NITs with an AIR greater than 40,000? ▾

Yes! With an AIR between 40,000 and 75,000, candidates regularly secure branches like Civil, Metallurgy, Chemical, Production, or Mining at top and mid-tier NITs (such as NIT Rourkela, NIT Durgapur, NIT Jalandhar, NIT Raipur), or Computer Science at northeastern NITs under Home State quotas, especially in CSAB rounds.
