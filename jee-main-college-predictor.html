Counselling Policy

Home State (HS) vs. Other State (OS) Quotas

National Institutes of Technology (NITs) allocate 50% of their total seats under the Home State Quota. The remaining 50% are filled via the Other State Quota.

Home State Quota Eligibility Rule

Your Home State is determined by the location of the school where you completed your Class 12 (or equivalent) board examination. It has nothing to do with your place of birth or permanent residence. For example, if you reside in Delhi but completed Class 12 in Uttar Pradesh, your official Home State for JoSAA is Uttar Pradesh.

Impact on Closing Cutoff Ranks

In most states (with exceptions like Delhi or Goa where competition is fierce), the closing ranks for the Home State Quota are significantly more relaxed than the Other State Quota. Selecting your Home State inside our predictor reveals these exclusive seats.

JoSAA vs. CSAB Counselling Mechanics

Admissions to public engineering institutes in India happen in two successive phases: JoSAA rounds and CSAB special rounds.

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority)

JoSAA conducts the first 5-6 rounds of counselling for all 23 IITs, 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and 30+ GFTIs. Every student registers once and locks their preference list. Category seats and gender-neutral/female-only supernumerary seats are allocated strictly by merit in successive rounds.

CSAB Special Rounds

After JoSAA rounds conclude, any seat vacant in NITs, IIITs, or GFTIs due to withdrawals or empty allocations are transferred to the Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB). CSAB conducts 2 Special Rounds. Note: **IIT seats are NOT part of CSAB rounds.**

JoSAA Seat Acceptance: Float vs. Freeze vs. Slide

Crucial guidelines to protect your allocated seat while remaining eligible for better branch upgrades.

Freeze (Accept & Exit)

Confirmed

Choose Freeze if you are 100% satisfied with your allocated college and branch. You accept the seat, pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF), and will NOT participate in subsequent rounds for any upgrades.

Action: Download Provisional Seat Allotment Letter & upload documents for online verification.

Float (Upgrade Anywhere)

Recommended

Choose Float if you accept the current allocated seat as a backup, but wish to be considered for higher preference choices in ANY institute in subsequent rounds.

Guarantee: If you get a higher choice, the old seat is released. If not, your current seat remains 100% reserved!

Slide (Same College Upgrade)

Internal Upgrade

Choose Slide if you love the allocated institute (e.g. NIT Trichy) and want to be considered for higher academic programs ONLY within the same institute.

Best For: Candidates committed to a specific campus looking strictly for internal branch sliding.

Branch Selection Guide: CSE vs. ECE vs. Specialized Tech

Curricula overlap, tech placement eligibility, and career mobility across flagship disciplines.

Computer Science (CSE / IT)

Highest Demand

Core focus on algorithms, data structures, operating systems, compiler design, and software architecture. Highest placement volume and compensation ceiling.

  • FAANG & Product Recruiter Overlap: 100% Eligible
  • Average Package (Tier 1 NIT): ₹18 – ₹28 LPA
  • Key Recommendation: First choice if coding, product design, or software systems is your goal.

AI, Data Science & MnC

Fastest Growing

Combines rigorous higher mathematics, linear algebra, and probability with machine learning, deep learning, and algorithmic trading.

  • Quant & AI Research Overlap: Top preference for FinTech & HFTs
  • Average Package (Tier 1 NIT): ₹20 – ₹32 LPA
  • Key Recommendation: Ideal for mathematically gifted students targeting AI/ML research or algorithmic finance.

Electronics & Comm. (ECE)

Dual Domain

Covers semiconductors, VLSI chip design, embedded microcontrollers, and wireless communications. High versatility with dual career avenues.

  • Software Recruiting Overlap: ~85% - 90% tech firms allow ECE
  • Semiconductor Boom: High growth in VLSI (Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, Nvidia)
  • Key Recommendation: The best alternative to CSE if missing top NIT computer science cutoffs.

Electrical, Mech & Chemical

Core Foundation

Fundamental industrial disciplines powering EVs, clean energy, industrial automation, robotics, aerospace, and public sector undertakings (PSUs).

  • PSU Eligibility via GATE: Direct recruitment by IOCL, ONGC, NTPC, BHEL
  • Tier 1 Campus Advantage: Top NIT branding allows non-tech consulting & finance placements
  • Key Recommendation: Choose a Top NIT Core branch over a Tier 3 local private college CSE.

Counselling Verification

JoSAA Mandatory Reporting & Document Checklist

Online Reporting Protocol

Upon receiving a provisional seat allotment in any JoSAA round, you must complete online reporting, upload original scanned documents, and pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) within 3-4 days. Failure to do so leads to permanent cancellation of your allocated seat.

1. Identity & Exam Credentials
  • Provisional Seat Allotment Letter (downloaded from JoSAA portal)
  • JEE Main Admit Card & Official NTA Scorecard
  • Class 10 Board Certificate / Birth Certificate (Date of birth proof)
  • Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID)
2. Academic Eligibility Proof
  • Class 12 Marksheet & Passing Certificate (Original scanned copy)
  • Proof of 75% aggregate marks (65% for SC/ST/PwD) OR Top 20 percentile certificate
  • Migration Certificate & School Transfer Certificate (for physical reporting)
3. Category & Medical Certificates
  • OBC-NCL / Gen-EWS Certificate: Must be issued on or after April 1 of the admission year in standard Central Govt format
  • SC / ST Certificate: Valid state/central caste certificate
  • Medical Certificate: In official JoSAA Annexure 7 format signed by registered doctor
  • Crossed bank cheque or passbook copy (for fee refund processing if withdrawing)
Financial Aid Framework

NITs Fee Structure & 100% Tuition Fee Remission Rules

Ministry of Education Scheme

All 31 National Institutes of Technology (NITs) operate under a standardized Central Government tuition fee structure with generous income-linked fee waivers:

100% TUITION WAIVER
₹0 Tuition Fee

Awarded automatically to: 1) All SC, ST, and PwD candidates, and 2) General and OBC candidates whose annual family income is less than ₹1 Lakh per annum.

66.67% (2/3RD) WAIVER
Pay Only 1/3rd Tuition

General and OBC candidates whose gross annual parental income is between ₹1 Lakh and ₹5 Lakh per annum receive a 2/3rd reduction on tuition fees (~₹20,833 per semester).

STANDARD SLAB
₹62,500 / Semester

General and OBC candidates with family income exceeding ₹5 Lakh pay the full standard tuition fee of ₹62,500/semester (plus hostel and mess charges).

College Predictor FAQs

Answers to common questions about cutoffs, seat quotas, verification, and branch options.

What is a mock seat allocation? ▼
Before the actual rounds begin, JoSAA runs two mock seat allocation rounds based on the initial choices locked by students. This helps you understand what college and branch you are likely to get, allowing you to reorder your choices before the final deadline.
Can I get into a top NIT with 95 percentile? ▼
Under the General category (OPEN), getting a top branch (like CSE/ECE) in top-tier NITs (like NIT Trichy or NIT Surathkal) is very difficult at 95 percentile. However, you can secure seats in lower branches (like Civil, Metallurgy, or Biotechnology) in those NITs, or core branches in newer NITs and GFTIs, especially if you qualify for the Home State Quota.
What is a GFTI? ▼
GFTIs (Government Funded Technical Institutes) are public engineering colleges funded by the Central Government or State Governments (such as BIT Mesra, PEC Chandigarh, and JNU School of Engineering). They participate in JoSAA/CSAB counselling and accept admissions via JEE Main ranks.
What is the difference between Float, Freeze, and Slide in JoSAA? ▼
Freeze accepts the allocated seat and withdraws from further rounds. Float accepts the seat but keeps you eligible for higher preference choices in any institute in subsequent rounds. Slide accepts the seat but permits upgrades only to a higher preference branch within the SAME institute.
How is Home State quota determined in NITs? ▼
Home State quota (50% of NIT seats) is strictly determined by the state or union territory where you completed your Class 12 (or equivalent) qualifying examination, regardless of your permanent home address or domicile certificate.
What are CSAB Special Rounds and who is eligible? ▼
CSAB conducts 2 Special Rounds after JoSAA Round 5/6 to fill remaining vacancies in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs (not IITs). All candidates with a valid JEE Main rank who have not retained an IIT seat are eligible to participate.
What happens if my OBC-NCL or Gen-EWS certificate was issued before April 1? ▼
JoSAA strictly mandates that OBC-NCL and Gen-EWS category certificates must be issued on or after April 1 of the admission year. If your certificate is older, JoSAA will raise an online verification query. If you fail to upload a valid certificate or an undertaking within the resolution window, your category will be automatically converted to General (OPEN), and your allocated reserved seat will be cancelled.
Can I participate in CSAB Special Rounds if I already accepted a seat in JoSAA? ▼
Yes! If you accept and confirm a seat in an NIT, IIIT, or GFTI during JoSAA, you can register for CSAB Special Rounds while retaining your JoSAA seat as a guaranteed backup. If you are allotted a higher choice in CSAB, your previous JoSAA seat is automatically released; if not, you retain your original JoSAA seat.
What is the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) and Partial Admission Fee (PAF)? ▼
The Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) is paid once upon receiving your first seat allotment in JoSAA (~₹35,000 for Gen/OBC/EWS, ~₹17,500 for SC/ST/PwD). After JoSAA Round 5 concludes, candidates retaining an NIT+ seat must pay the Partial Admission Fee (PAF, ~₹40,000) on the JoSAA portal to confirm their admission before physical reporting. Both fees are adjusted against your 1st semester institute tuition fee.
Is physical reporting at the allotted institute mandatory after online JoSAA rounds? ▼
Yes. After online document verification and fee payment, you must physically report to your final allotted campus during the designated reporting dates (usually late July or early August). You will undergo original physical document verification, hostel room allocation, and attend the institute orientation.
How do internal branch sliding and upgrades work after joining an NIT? ▼
Most NITs offer internal branch sliding at the end of the 1st year (2nd semester) based strictly on your 1st-year CGPA (typically requiring a 8.5+ to 9.2+ CGPA to slide into CSE or ECE), subject to seat vacancy in the target department. No backlogs are permitted.
