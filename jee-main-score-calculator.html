Official Rules

NTA Marking Scheme & Negative Marking Rules

Complete official scoring framework for Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) covering Section A and Section B.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) computes raw marks across 3 subjects: Physics (100 Marks), Chemistry (100 Marks), and Mathematics (100 Marks), totaling 300 maximum marks. Each subject consists of 2 sections: Section A (20 Multiple Choice Questions) and Section B (5 Numerical Value Questions).

Response Type Section A (20 MCQs) Section B (5 Numerical) Total Marks Impact
Correct Response +4 Marks +4 Marks Adds +4 marks directly to raw score
Incorrect Response -1 Mark -1 Mark Deducts -1 mark penalty from raw score
Unattempted / Dropped 0 Marks 0 Marks No score addition or penalty
Question Dropped by NTA +4 to All +4 to Attempted Full marks credited based on NTA policy
⚠️
Section B Negative Marking Rule
  • In earlier years, Section B (Numerical value questions) carried zero negative marking. NTA has since enforced a strict -1 penalty on all incorrect numerical questions.
  • Random numerical guessing carries a severe negative expected value and will rapidly depress your final percentile.
  • Numerical answers must be rounded off to the nearest integer as indicated in the question instructions.
🎯
Mathematical Formula for Raw Marks
  • Raw Score Formula:
    $$\text{Total Score} = (\text{Total Correct} \times 4) - (\text{Total Incorrect} \times 1)$$
  • Theoretical Range: From -75 (if all 75 questions are wrong) to +300 (all 75 questions correct).
  • Accuracy Impact: A student who attempts 50 questions with 48 correct scores 190 marks, while an aspirant who attempts 70 questions with 48 correct scores only 170 marks (a 20-mark drop due to 22 negative marks!).
Step-by-Step Procedure

How to Check Your Response Sheet & Answer Key

Learn how to download your recorded responses and cross-verify with NTA provisional keys.

📥
Downloading Recorded Responses
  • 1. Visit the official NTA website: jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  • 2. Click on the active link: "Display of Question Paper and Recorded Responses".
  • 3. Log in with your Application Number and Password (or Date of Birth).
  • 4. Download the Response Sheet HTML/PDF and save a copy immediately, as NTA deactivates the link within 2 to 3 days.
🔍
Matching Question ID & Option ID
  • NTA randomizes questions and options for every examinee to prevent cheating. Do NOT look for Question 1, 2, 3 in order!
  • Locate the unique Question ID printed on your response sheet.
  • Find the corresponding Question ID in the official Answer Key PDF.
  • Compare your Chosen Option ID with the Correct Option ID given by NTA.
  • If they match: tally +4. If they do not match: tally -1. If you did not answer: tally 0.
Discrepancy Redressal

Answer Key Challenge Process & Score Adjustments

What to do if you believe an official NTA answer key contains a factual error.

If you find a genuine discrepancy between the provisional key and standard NCERT textbooks, you can submit an official challenge on the NTA candidate portal within the specified 48-hour objection window.

⚖️
Challenge Guidelines & Fees
  • Processing Fee: A non-refundable processing fee of ₹200 per question challenged must be paid online via debit card, credit card, or UPI.
  • Supporting Evidence: You must upload a consolidated PDF document containing textbook citations, derivations, or NCERT page scans supporting your claim.
  • Expert Panel Review: A panel of subject matter experts reviews all candidate challenges. If a challenge is upheld, the key is finalized and updated.
📈
Impact of Dropped Questions
  • Dropped Section A Question: Full +4 marks are credited to ALL candidates who appeared in that shift, regardless of whether they attempted it or not.
  • Dropped Section B Question: Full +4 marks are credited only to candidates who attempted the question (since there is a choice to attempt 5 out of 10).
  • Multiple Correct Options: If more than one option is declared correct by NTA, +4 marks are awarded to candidates who selected any one of the correct options.
