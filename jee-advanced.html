What is JEE Advanced?

India's most intellectually rigorous entrance examination and the sole route to 23 Indian Institutes of Technology.

Joint Entrance Examination (Advanced) is organized under the directives of the Joint Admission Board (JAB) by one of the seven designated zonal IITs (IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Kanpur, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Madras, or IIT Roorkee) on a rotational basis. It serves as the exclusive examination for admission to undergraduate programs—including 4-Year B.Tech, 5-Year Integrated Dual Degree B.Tech-M.Tech, and BS-MS degrees—across all 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

🏛️
Participating Institutes
  • 23 IITs: All undergraduate engineering and scientific research seats across old and new IITs are allocated solely through JEE Advanced ranks.
  • Premier Allied Research Institutes: IISc Bangalore, IIPE Visakhapatnam, RGIPT Amethi, and IISERs accept JEE Advanced scores through dedicated direct channels.
  • Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT): Qualifiers can write the AAT to enter the 5-year B.Arch programs at IIT BHU, IIT Kharagpur, and IIT Roorkee.
⚡
Key Unique Attributes
  • Both Papers Compulsory: Candidates must write both Paper 1 (Morning) and Paper 2 (Afternoon) on the same day. Missing either paper results in disqualification.
  • Unannounced Dynamic Pattern: Unlike JEE Main, the total marks, number of questions, and scoring schemes change every year and are revealed only inside the exam hall.
  • Zero Home State Quota: 100% of seats across all 23 IITs are open strictly on an All India Quota (AI) basis.

⚖️
One or More Than One Correct (Multi-Choice)
  • +4 Marks if all correct options are selected and no incorrect option is marked.
  • +3, +2, or +1 Partial Marks if only some of the correct options are marked and no incorrect option is selected.
  • -2 Marks Penalty if even a single incorrect option is marked, regardless of whether any correct option was selected!
  • High Risk Area: Guesswork in multi-correct questions is devastating due to the heavy -2 negative penalty.
🔢
Numerical & Other Question Formats
  • Numerical Value Questions: Answers can be non-negative integers or decimal values entered via on-screen virtual keypad (+3 or +4 for correct, 0 or -1 for incorrect depending on the year).
  • Single Correct MCQs: +3 marks for correct choice, -1 mark for incorrect choice.
  • Matrix-Match & Matching Lists: Matching Column I items with Column II (+3 for all matching pairs, -1 for error).
  • Comprehension Paragraphs: 2 to 3 conceptual questions based on a scientific passage or experimental setup.
Candidacy Rules

Eligibility Criteria & The 2-Attempt Limitation

Strict eligibility prerequisites established by the Joint Admission Board (JAB).

🎯
Top 2,50,000 JEE Main Qualification
  • Must rank within the top 2,50,000 successful candidates in B.E./B.Tech Paper 1 of JEE Main across all categories.
  • Category Quota Breakdown:
    • Open (GEN): ~1,01,250 candidates
    • GEN-EWS: ~25,000 candidates
    • OBC-NCL: ~67,500 candidates
    • SC: ~37,500 candidates
    • ST: ~18,750 candidates
    • PwD: 5% horizontal reservation within each category.
⏱️
Strict 2-Attempt Rule & Age Limit
  • Only 2 Consecutive Years: You can attempt JEE Advanced for a maximum of 2 consecutive times starting from the year you first appear for Class 12 board examinations.
  • No Drops Beyond Class 12 + 1 Year: If you completed Class 12 in 2025, you can attempt in 2025 and 2026 only. You are NOT eligible in 2027.
  • Prior IIT Admission Bar: A candidate who previously accepted an IIT seat and reported to an institute cannot write JEE Advanced again.
  • 75% Board Rule: Minimum 75% marks (65% for SC/ST) in Class 12 OR top-20 percentile of qualifying board.
Depth vs Breadth

Syllabus Expectations & Multi-Concept Problems

JEE Advanced tests interdisciplinary concepts across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

⚡
Physics Advanced Focus
  • Rotational Dynamics & Rigid Body Mechanics: Rolling without slipping, angular momentum conservation, collision with hinges.
  • Electrodynamics: Electromagnetic Induction (Lenz law, motional EMF), variable capacitance, LC oscillations, Maxwell equations.
  • Thermodynamics & Fluids: Viscosity, Bernoulli equation, Carnot cycles, heat transfer by conduction & radiation.
  • Wave Optics: Polarization, interference with glass slabs, resolving power of optical instruments.
🧪
Chemistry Advanced Focus
  • Organic Reaction Mechanisms: Multi-step organic synthesis, stereochemistry (R/S, optical isomerism), carbocation rearrangements.
  • Inorganic Qualitative Analysis: Salt analysis tests, precipitation colors, coordination isomerism and CFT orbital splitting.
  • Physical Chemistry Numericals: Electrochemistry (concentration cells), ionic equilibrium (solubility product with common ion effect), chemical thermodynamics.
📐
Mathematics Advanced Focus & Problem Solving Depth
Calculus Mastery
  • Definite Integrals with functional equations
  • Area under multiple intersecting curves
  • Differential Equations with integrating factors
  • Continuity & Differentiability theorems (LMVT, Rolle)
Algebra & Probability
  • Complex Numbers with geometry (concyclic points)
  • Matrices & System of linear equations
  • Combinatorics, Multinomial theorem & derangements
  • Conditional Probability & Bayes theorem problems
Geometry & Vectors
  • Vectors (Scalar & Vector Triple Products)
  • 3D Coordinate Geometry (Shortest distance between skew lines)
  • Conic Sections (Normal & tangent properties of parabola, ellipse, hyperbola)
Qualification Thresholds

Minimum Subject-Wise & Aggregate Qualifying Marks

Scoring high in aggregate is not enough: candidates must clear individual subject cutoff thresholds.

To be included in the official Common Rank List (CRL) and category rank lists, every candidate must clear two separate thresholds: 1. Minimum marks in EACH subject individually (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics combined across Paper 1 and Paper 2), AND 2. Minimum aggregate score across both papers combined.

Rank List Category Minimum Marks in Each Subject Minimum Total Aggregate Marks Historical CRL Cutoff Percentage
Common Rank List (CRL) 10% of Subject Full Score (~12 marks) 35% of Total Full Score (~109 – 126 marks) ~30% – 35%
GEN-EWS Rank List 9% of Subject Full Score (~10 marks) 31.5% of Total Full Score (~98 – 114 marks) ~27% – 31%
OBC-NCL Rank List 9% of Subject Full Score (~10 marks) 31.5% of Total Full Score (~98 – 114 marks) ~27% – 31%
SC / ST Rank Lists 5% of Subject Full Score (~6 marks) 17.5% of Total Full Score (~54 – 63 marks) ~15% – 18%
PwD Rank Lists (All categories) 5% of Subject Full Score (~6 marks) 17.5% of Total Full Score (~54 – 63 marks) ~15% – 18%
Aggregate Score = (Physics P1 + P2) + (Chemistry P1 + P2) + (Mathematics P1 + P2)
If an aspirant scores 110 marks in Physics and 110 marks in Chemistry, but only 5 marks in Mathematics (below the subject threshold), they are disqualified from all IIT rank lists, despite having a massive 225 total aggregate score!

Counseling & Admission

JoSAA Counseling & 23 IITs Seat Allocation

How the Joint Seat Allocation Authority matches candidate choices with institute merit lists.

1
Centralized JoSAA Portal
Register with your JEE Advanced Roll Number. Fill and order unlimited choices of branches across all 23 IITs.
2
Strict Merit Matching
Seats are allocated strictly based on Common Rank List (CRL) and category ranks without any state quota interference.
3
Freeze, Float, or Slide
Freeze your current seat, or opt for Float (seeking a higher preference anywhere in IITs) or Slide (higher branch in same IIT).
4
Online Reporting & Fee
Upload documents (Class 12 marksheet, medical certificate, category certificate) and pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF).
5
Final Physical Reporting
After Round 6, complete in-person campus registration and hostel allocation at your designated IIT.
👩‍🔬
Female Supernumerary Quota
  • Every undergraduate program across all 23 IITs maintains at least 20% female enrollment through supernumerary seats.
  • These seats are strictly additional and do not reduce the general seat pool available to male or gender-neutral applicants.
  • Closing ranks for female candidates in top branches (e.g. CSE, MnC, Electrical) are often significantly relaxed.
🚫
No CSAB Rounds for IITs
  • Vital Rule: CSAB does NOT conduct spot rounds for IIT seats!
  • Once JoSAA Round 6 finishes, admissions to all 23 IITs are permanently closed for that academic year.
  • Any unfilled IIT seats are offered only through preparatory courses for SC/ST candidates or forfeited.
  • Ensure all choice locking and seat acceptance procedures are completed during official JoSAA rounds.
