⚡ NTA Normalization Engine

Because JEE Main papers vary in difficulty across shifts, NTA converts raw marks into percentile scores. Our engine simulates:

  • Shift difficulty offsets (Hard, Moderate, Easy)
  • Session 1 (January) vs Session 2 (April) score inflation
  • Category-wise CRL rank multipliers for EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST
  • Female Supernumerary 20% seat quota adjustments
